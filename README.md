
## 📲 Install the Tennis App

The Tennis App can be added to your phone, tablet, or computer so you can launch it directly from your home screen or desktop, just like a normal app.

**🌐 Open the app:**  
https://youngmr290.github.io/Tennis-App/

No app store download is required!

### 🤖 Android (Phone or Tablet)

1. Open the Tennis App link in **Google Chrome**.
2. Tap the **three-dot menu (⋮)** in the top-right corner.
3. Select **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
4. Tap **Add** or **Install** to confirm.
5. The Tennis App will now appear on your home screen.

### 🍎 iPhone

1. Open the Tennis App link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an upward arrow).
3. Scroll down and select **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Enable **Open as Web App**, if available.
5. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.
6. The Tennis App will now appear on your home screen.

### 🍎 iPad

1. Open the Tennis App link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Select **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Enable **Open as Web App**, if available.
5. Tap **Add**.
6. Launch the Tennis App from your home screen.

### 💻 Windows (Chrome)

1. Open the Tennis App link in **Google Chrome**.
2. Click the **three-dot menu (⋮)**.
3. Select **Cast, save and share → Install page as app**, or **Create shortcut** if installation isn't available.
4. Follow the prompts to create the app or shortcut.
5. Pin it to your taskbar or Start menu for easy access.

### 💻 Windows (Microsoft Edge)

1. Open the Tennis App link in **Microsoft Edge**.
2. Click the **three-dot menu (⋯)**.
3. Select **Apps → Install this site as an app**.
4. Click **Install**.
5. Launch it from your desktop, taskbar or Start menu.

### 🖥️ Mac (Safari)

1. Open the Tennis App link in **Safari**.
2. Select **File → Add to Dock** (macOS Sonoma or later).
3. Give the app a name and click **Add**.
4. Launch the Tennis App directly from your Dock.

---

### ℹ️ Important Notes

- The app uses the same website, so updates are available when the online version is updated.
- An internet connection may still be required.
- Installing the app does not automatically synchronise saved data between devices.
- If your fixtures or player information are stored locally, clearing browser or app data may remove them.

**Tip:** Add the app to your home screen for quick access before each tennis session! 🎾
